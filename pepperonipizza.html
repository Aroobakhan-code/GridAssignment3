Pepperoni Pizza

Our Pepperoni Pizza is a true crowd favorite.
Hand-rolled dough is baked golden and topped with rich tomato sauce, stretchy mozzarella cheese and generous slices of
spicy pepperoni that turn crispy at the edges. Every bite is hot, cheesy and full of flavor. Perfect for sharing with family and friends, or enjoying all on your own.
