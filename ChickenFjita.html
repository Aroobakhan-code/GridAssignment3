Fajita Pizza

Our Chicken Fajita Pizza brings bold flavor to every slice. Hand-rolled dough is baked golden and loaded with tender, spicy marinated chicken, juicy onions, crisp green peppers and stretchy mozzarella cheese. A drizzle of our special creamy sauce ties it all together. Smoky, zesty and full of flavor, it is perfect for anyone who loves a little spice.
